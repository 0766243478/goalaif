03 · Verification states

SIREEN does not say “looks secure.”

CONFIRMEDForge verified. Evidence complete.
DEGRADEDVerification incomplete. Manual review required.
UNVERIFIEDHypothesis only. Nothing has executed.
FAILEDPipeline execution failed. No claim is made.
CLEAN_WITH_COVERAGENothing found within verified coverage.
04 · Core v0.1 scope

Built narrow on purpose.

SUPPORTED
  • One selected Solidity file
  • Heuristic or optional LLM-assisted hypotheses
  • Reentrancy, access-control, arithmetic PoC templates
  • Local Forge verification
  • Durable local audit history and Markdown/JSON reports
NOT YET
  • Repository-wide or multi-file analysis
  • Move auditing
  • Slither, Mythril, or Echidna in the core loop
  • Autonomous agents or guaranteed discovery
  • Cloud, teams, enterprise, or risk scoring
